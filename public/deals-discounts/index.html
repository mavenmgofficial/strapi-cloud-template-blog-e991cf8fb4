Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about military discounts

Who qualifies for military discounts?

+
Military discounts are typically available to active duty service members, veterans, retirees, National Guard, Reserves, and often military spouses and dependents. Eligibility varies by retailer, so we always list specific requirements on each discount page.

How do I verify my military status?

+
Most retailers use verification services like ID.me or SheerID. You'll create a free account and verify your status once, then discounts are automatically applied. Some stores also accept military IDs in person.

Can I use multiple discounts at once?

+
It depends on the retailer. Some military discounts can be "stacked" with sales and coupons, while others cannot. We indicate whether a discount is stackable on each listing.

Are these discounts verified?

+
Yes! Our team verifies every discount before listing it and regularly checks for accuracy. We also rely on community feedback to keep our database current. Look for the "Verified" badge on each listing.

How do I submit a discount I found?

+
We love community contributions! Click the "Submit a Discount" button in the navigation or at the bottom of this page. Fill out the simple form with the discount details and we'll verify and add it within 48 hours.
